Scarface Stego Studio is an offline Android steganography and media-forensics toolkit.

Features include encrypted message hiding and revealing in lossless PNG images and uncompressed PCM WAV audio, AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption, PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 password derivation, SHA-256 hashing, entropy analysis, media structure inspection, appended-data detection, file-signature indicators, and a read-only binary/hex lab.

The application performs processing locally on the device and does not request the Android INTERNET permission.

It is intended for legitimate privacy, education, forensic research, and inspection of files that you own or are authorised to analyse.

Discovered or extracted content is never automatically executed.
